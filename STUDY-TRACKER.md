- `NOT STARTED` — Work has not begun.
- `IN PROGRESS` — Currently studying or documenting.
- `NOTES COMPLETE` — Notes and required question answers are written.
- `LAB COMPLETE` — Required practical exercises are completed and documented.
- `REVIEW NEEDED` — Notes or evidence need correction or further verification.

## Question Documentation Checklist

For each question, verify the following where applicable:

- [ ] Question or learning objective recorded.
- [ ] Short answer written.
- [ ] Detailed answer explained in beginner-friendly language.
- [ ] Technical details verified.
- [ ] Commands and their options explained.
- [ ] Actual lab observations recorded when required.
- [ ] Expected results distinguished from observed results.
- [ ] Key takeaways and revision notes added.
- [ ] Relevant references included.
- [ ] Answer reviewed for accuracy.

## Completion Rules

A module must not be marked `COMPLETE` until its required learning objectives and questions have been addressed, the applicable practical exercises have been completed and documented, and the resulting notes have been reviewed.

If a module has no required practical exercise, its lab status may be marked `NOT APPLICABLE` with an explanation.

Question counts must reflect actual documented questions, not estimates.

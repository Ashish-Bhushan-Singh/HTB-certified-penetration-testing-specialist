# 📋 Section 07: Pre-Engagement

**Module:** 01 — Penetration Testing Process  
**Topic:** Pre-Engagement Documentation

---

## 📚 Overview

The pre-engagement phase establishes the legal, operational, and contractual foundation for a penetration test. It helps ensure that the client and penetration testing team agree on the assessment's scope, responsibilities, limitations, and objectives before testing begins.

Proper documentation helps prevent misunderstandings, defines authorization, and provides a reference throughout the engagement.

---

## ❓ Questions & Answers

### Q1. How many documents must be prepared in total for a penetration test?

#### ✅ Short Answer

**Answer: 7 documents.**

#### 📝 Detailed Explanation

According to the provided learning material, **seven types of documents** may be prepared throughout a penetration testing engagement.

| No. | Document | When It Is Prepared | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) | After initial contact | Establishes confidentiality obligations for sensitive information shared during the engagement. |
| 2 | Scoping Questionnaire | Before the pre-engagement meeting | Collects initial information about the client's environment, objectives, and requirements. |
| 3 | Scoping Document | During the pre-engagement meeting | Helps define the assessment's scope, including relevant systems, boundaries, and requirements. |
| 4 | Penetration Testing Proposal / Contract / Statement of Work (SoW) | During the pre-engagement meeting | Documents the proposed services, deliverables, responsibilities, and agreed scope of work. |
| 5 | Rules of Engagement (RoE) | Before the kick-off meeting | Establishes the permitted testing activities, restrictions, communication procedures, and operational boundaries. |
| 6 | Contractor's Agreement | Before the kick-off meeting, for physical assessments | Addresses applicable responsibilities and agreements involving contractors participating in physical security assessments. |
| 7 | Reports | During and after the penetration test | Documents findings, supporting evidence, security impact, and recommendations. |

**Important:** The list identifies seven document types, not necessarily seven individual files. For example, reporting may involve multiple reports or deliverables depending on the engagement.

### 🔍 Why These Documents Matter

These documents help the penetration testing team and client establish a shared understanding before testing starts.

They serve several important purposes:

- **Confidentiality:** Protects sensitive information exchanged during the engagement.
- **Planning:** Collects the information needed to prepare an appropriate assessment.
- **Scope definition:** Identifies which systems and activities are included or excluded.
- **Authorization:** Helps establish the boundaries within which testing is permitted.
- **Coordination:** Defines responsibilities, communication channels, and operational constraints.
- **Accountability:** Provides a documented basis for reporting findings and evaluating the engagement.

The precise documents required depend on the engagement, the organization's policies, and applicable legal or contractual requirements.
